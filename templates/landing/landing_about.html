About Us

At WiadSpot, we’re redefining connectivity in public spaces. We deliver free, fast, and secure Wi-Fi while unlocking high-intent ad moments through a privacy-respecting captive portal.

  • • Privacy-first analytics and consented engagement
  • • Resilient networks for outdoor & high-footfall venues
  • • Self-serve advertiser tools & real-time dashboards

Our Goal

To make public Wi-Fi effortless for venues and meaningful for users—turning every connection into a trusted, value-driven experience.

  • Reliable Access: Seamless onboarding with OTP/social options.
  • Respect for Privacy: Clear consent flows and transparent controls.
  • Win-Win Monetization: Context-aware ads that fund free Wi-Fi.
  • Useful Insights: Venue trends without exposing identities.

What we’re building

A platform that cafés, clinics, libraries, bus hubs and malls can deploy in minutes. Operators run networks safely; advertisers reach audiences respectfully; people get fast, free internet.
