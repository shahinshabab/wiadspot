Monetizing Wi-Fi for Venues

Free Wi-Fi can pay for itself through smart models aligned with guest experience.

Models

  • Sponsored Access: a brand subsidizes connectivity for exposure.
  • Self-serve Local Ads: nearby businesses promote time-bound offers.
  • Premium Tiers: upsell higher speeds or longer sessions.

Where to Start

  • Define audience and peak times; price by impressions and dwell.
  • Use simple formats (static banner + one CTA) for speed and reliability.
  • Share transparent reports: sessions, reach, and redemptions.
