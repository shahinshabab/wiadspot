OTP vs Social Login in Captive Portals

Both reduce friction compared to long forms—but they fit different goals.

OTP Login

  • Pros: simple, trusted, verified phone; good conversion.
  • Cons: SMS cost; phone-number handling obligations.

Social Login

  • Pros: richer profile attributes; fewer typos.
  • Cons: consent complexity; platform policy changes.

What to Choose?

  • Run an A/B test by venue type; optimize for completion and support load.
  • Offer a fallback (voucher/help desk) for users without phone or social accounts.
