The Wi-Fi Marketing Playbook

Login moments are high-intent. Done right, they create value for guests and measurable outcomes for venues.

Core Tactics

  • Welcome Offers: coupon or loyalty join after consent.
  • First-party Signals: short surveys (1–2 questions) for preferences.
  • Remarketing: privacy-safe channels (email/WhatsApp with opt-in).

Creative Tips

  • Keep copy four lines max on mobile; one clear CTA.
  • Use venue-relevant imagery and seasonality (festivals, sports, events).
  • Track uplift: impressions → clicks → store actions (QR scans, redemptions).

Measure What Matters

  • Unique sessions, new vs returning, dwell time, offer redemptions.
  • Attribute responsibly; avoid over-collecting personal data.
