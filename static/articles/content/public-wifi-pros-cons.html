Public Wi-Fi: Pros and Cons for Venues and Users

Public Wi-Fi boosts footfall and engagement, but it introduces duty-of-care and privacy considerations.

Pros

  • Venue value: longer dwell time, repeat visits, and feedback channels.
  • User value: connectivity for work, study, travel, and entertainment.
  • City value: digital inclusion, tourism support, and smart services.

Cons

  • Abuse risk: heavy downloads, prohibited content.
  • Privacy: collection and handling of personal data requires care.
  • Support: captive portals can confuse first-time users.

Mitigations

  • Rate limits, time quotas, and content filtering.
  • Clear privacy notice; minimize data; anonymize & aggregate analytics.
  • Simple, resilient login (OTP or voucher) and a visible help link.
